# THE WORLD — Tuesday 24 November 2026

One day of world news, one picture: a full newspaper front page for Tuesday
24 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (Python + PIL) so every headline
stays perfectly legible.

**44 date-checked stories · 8 regions — every one happened or was first
reported on this Tuesday.**

- 6 illustrated lead stories: Asia's markets rocket in a chip rally,
  a dawn earthquake shakes Nepal, a Russian strike kills two in
  Zaporizhzhia, Seoul sounds an air alert over a Russian jet, a Himalayan
  flood leaves 100 missing, and America takes off for Thanksgiving.
- 38 numbered briefs across 8 regions, each with a colour-coded region tag.
- A region key with story counts, a round-the-world timeline of the day,
  and a footer stating the date-check rule.
